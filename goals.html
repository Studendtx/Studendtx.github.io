Goals: my SWOT analysis

17 September 2026

A SWOT analysis looks at my internal strengths and weaknesses and the external opportunities and threats that affect my study.

Strengths Weaknesses

1. Hands-on with hardware: I have been building computers since I was 13. Not afraid to experiment with Linux, modding and new software.

2. Persistent: when something does not work, I keep going until it does.

3. Collaborative: I work well in groups or in teams.

1. I start a lot of projects and do not always finish them (like my Minecraft client). Little experience with structured programming so far.

2. I can be forgetful.
Opportunities Threats

1. Broadness of the first year at HZ lets me try different parts of ICT before I choose.

2. Security and privacy, my main interest, is a growing field with a lot of jobs.

1. I have a lot of hobbies that can take time away from studying.

2. Getting distracted by social media when I should be studying.

Goal 1: finish what I start

Based on: weakness 1 (unfinished projects) and threat 1 (hobbies taking time).

By 31 January 2027 I will finish and publish one small security project on my GitHub, for example a password strength checker, with a readme.md that explains what it does and what I learned. I will work on it for 2 hours every week, and keep track of my progress so I can see if I fall behind.

Goal 2: build a security foundation

Based on: opportunity 2 (security is a growing field) and strength 2 (persistent).

Before the end of Q2 (April 2027) I will complete the TryHackMe Pre-Security learning path and write a blog post on this website about what I learned and what I found difficult.

How I will reach them

Each weekday, I will complete two focused ~45-minute sessions on important tasks. I will eliminate distractions by turning off notifications and keeping unrelated tabs closed, which tackles threat 2 (social media). I will write down distracting thoughts and return my attention to the task. If I encounter a problem, I will attempt to solve it independently while documenting my steps, failures, and questions. If I cannot solve it within 30 minutes, I will contact a teacher and share my notes for guidance.
