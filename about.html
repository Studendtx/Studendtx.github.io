About me

I am a 19-year-old guy from Arnemuiden. I've always had an affinity for technology, starting with iPads, tablets and drones when I was about 9. Since my mom didn't want to buy Minecraft Pocket Edition for me, I had to find a different way to obtain the game. This led me down a rabbit hole of APK's, questionable websites, and a lot of viruses. I persisted, and managed to install the game for free. I definitely gave my tablet a couple of viruses, but to younger me, that was not as important as Minecraft.
