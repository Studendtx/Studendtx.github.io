Side B

I want to understand how things really work, and where they break.

How
By being hands-on: building, breaking and fixing things until I understand them.
What
Studying HBO-ICT at the HZ, and growing towards a career in cybersecurity.

Where I'm headed.

Security and privacy interest me most. After graduating I want to work in cybersecurity, as a security specialist or pentester: testing systems and networks for weaknesses, and writing clear reports that help a company fix them.

The full plan

  1. Year one. A solid base in programming, networks and data. You cannot secure what you do not understand.
  2. Year two. Software Engineering, then a specialisation in security or infrastructure.
  3. Outside class. TryHackMe and beginner CTF challenges, starting with the Pre-Security path by April 2027.
  4. Next. An entry-level certification such as CompTIA Security+, and an internship with a security team.
