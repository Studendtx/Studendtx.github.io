Direction: where I see myself in the future

24 September 2026

Security and privacy interest me most. I like understanding something well enough to see where it could break. I hope to start working in the ICT sector as soon as possible, to gain experience.

Why security?

Looking back, almost everything I did with technology led me here. Getting Minecraft on my tablet as a kid taught me (the hard way) what malware is. Building a 'utility' client for anarchy servers showed me how easily software can be bent to do things it was not meant to do. After that I started securing my own devices, trying out TailsOS and jailbreaking my 3DS. Security is the one part of ICT where my curiosity about how things break becomes something useful: finding the weak spot before someone with bad intentions does.

How I will get there

  • Use the broad first year to build a solid base in programming, networks and data, since you cannot secure what you do not understand.
  • Choose Software Engineering in year 2
  • Choose a specialisation focused on security or infrastructure when the time comes.
  • Practise outside of class with platforms like TryHackMe and beginner CTF (Capture The Flag) challenges.
  • Work towards an entry-level certification such as CompTIA Security+.
  • Look for an internship at a company with a security team.

Future me: in five years

After graduating I would like to work in cybersecurity, for example as a security specialist or pentester, at a company where I can keep learning and developing new skills. In five years I see myself testing systems and networks for weaknesses and writing clear reports that help a company fix them. I also want to use AI in my work, because it is quickly becoming important on both sides of security: attackers use it, so defenders need to understand it too.
