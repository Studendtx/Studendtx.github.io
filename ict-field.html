The ICT field of work

16 October 2026

The ICT field is evolving rapidly, largely due to the rise of AI and its growing role in modern technology. AI is changing how software is developed, how businesses operate and how problems are solved, making knowledge of AI increasingly valuable for ICT professionals. Although AI can automate certain programming tasks, it does not eliminate the need for software engineers. Instead, demand is shifting towards professionals who can work with AI, develop complex systems, and maintain secure and reliable software. While some entry-level programming jobs may face increased competition, skilled ICT professionals remain essential as digitalisation continues to expand across industries.

My goal is to get familiar with AI on a professional level, allowing me to use it to my and my company's advantage. In order to achieve this I am planning on deep diving into Machine Learning, LLM's and other different use cases for AI agents, aswell as making sure to build some experience through using it with certain personal projects I might do in the future.
