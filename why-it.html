Why did I choose ICT?

What I know about the field

I have always been busy with technology in some way, shape or form. I started out building computers at age 13. Hardware-wise, I kept up with the release of new graphics cards and processors. At some point I started taking a larger interest in the software side of things aswell. This is when I started experimenting with Linux and dual-booting it. I also started working on an 'utility' client for Minecraft Java, to use on anarchy servers which I was playing on at the time. Although I quit working on this relatively soon, it did cause my interest in the security and privacy side of things to rise. I started optimizing all of my devices for privacy and efficiency, and later on started experimenting with things like TailsOS, jailbreaking and modding devices, like my 3DS. Due to my interest in all things music, I began using FL Studio aswell.

My motivation

I want to understand how the internet and the devices I use every day actually work, not just how to use them. ICT is a very broad field, and that is exactly why it appeals to me: the first year gives me a solid foundation, so I can choose a direction later based on real knowledge.

My expectations

I expect a lot of hands-on work: building things, breaking them and fixing them. I also expect it to be harder than tinkering at home, because now there are different requirements and other people who rely on my work.

What I want to learn

  • Writing clean, readable code instead of code that only just works.
  • How networks and the internet work under the hood.
  • More about security and privacy, the thing that got me into this in the first place.
  • Working in a team on a real project.
